Privacy Policy
Effective Date: May 13, 2025
App Name: Prayer Mate
App Name: All My Gigs
Developer: parteSoft

1. Introduction

At parteSoft, we respect your privacy. This policy describes what information our apps may 
collect, how it is used, and the choices you have. This applies to All My Gigs and Prayer Mate.

2. Information We Collect

a. Personal Information

We do not collect, store, or share personally identifiable information such as your name, email, 
address, or phone number.

b. Location Data (All My Gigs only)

If you grant permission, All My Gigs may access your device’s location to display real-time speed, 
direction, and temperature.

Location data is processed only on your device and is not stored or transmitted to our servers.

c. Device and App Usage Data (All My Gigs only)

The app may locally use device and app activity to:

Filter incoming gig requests

Launch assigned gig apps

This data stays on your device and is not sent to us.

d. Advertising Data (All My Gigs free version only)

We use Google AdMob for ads in the free version. AdMob may collect:

Device identifiers

IP address

General location (approximate)
This is used only for delivering and personalizing ads per Google’s Privacy Policy.

e. In-App Purchases

We use Google Play Billing to process upgrades to the premium version. Payment details are 
handled directly by Google and are not collected or stored by us.

f. Prayer Mate

Prayer Mate does not collect, store, or share any information and does not contain ads or third-party tracking.

3. How We Use Information

On-device processing for gig filtering, speed/direction display, and app launching (All My Gigs only).

Ad delivery through AdMob in the free version of All My Gigs.

In-app purchase processing via Google Play Billing.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell or share your personal data.
Third-party services that may process limited data:

Google AdMob (ads in free version of All My Gigs)

Google Play Billing (handling premium upgrades)

Each third party has its own privacy policy.

5. Data Retention

We do not store personal data on our servers.

All user preferences and app-related data remain on your device.

AdMob and Google Play Billing may retain data per their respective privacy policies.

6. Your Choices

You can disable location permissions in your device settings at any time.

You can upgrade to the premium version to remove ads.

You can uninstall the app to delete all locally stored data.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as needed. Any changes will be posted on this GitHub page and reflected in the “Effective Date” above.
