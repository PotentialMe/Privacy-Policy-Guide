# Privacy Policy — parteSoft Apps

**Effective Date:** June 10, 2026  
**Developer:** parteSoft

**Apps covered by this policy:**
- All My Gigs
- Prayer Mate
- Study With Me
- VitalSyncFlow

---

## 1. Introduction

At parteSoft, we respect your privacy. This policy describes what information our apps may collect, how it is used, and the choices you have. It applies to all apps listed above.

We do not operate our own servers to collect or store your personal data. Unless stated otherwise for a specific feature, information stays on your device or is handled directly by third-party services you choose to use (such as Google AdMob or Google Play Billing).

---

## 2. Information We Collect

### a. Personal Information (general)

We do not collect, store, or share personally identifiable information such as your name, email, address, or phone number through our own systems.

Some apps let **you** enter content (study notes, patient names, shift notes, etc.). That content is stored **locally on your device** unless a feature below explicitly sends it to a third party.

### b. Location Data (All My Gigs only)

If you grant permission, All My Gigs may access your device’s location to display real-time speed, direction, and temperature.

Location data is processed only on your device and is not stored or transmitted to our servers.

### c. Device and App Usage Data (All My Gigs only)

The app may locally use device and app activity to:

- Filter incoming gig requests
- Launch assigned gig apps

This data stays on your device and is not sent to us.

### d. Advertising Data (All My Gigs, Study With Me, VitalSyncFlow)

We use **Google AdMob** to show banner ads in these apps (unless ads have been removed as described below). AdMob may collect:

- Device identifiers (including advertising ID where permitted)
- IP address
- General location (approximate)
- App interaction and diagnostic data related to ad delivery

This is used only for delivering and measuring ads per [Google’s Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

**Study With Me:** You can remove banner ads with a one-time in-app purchase.  
**All My Gigs:** You can remove ads by upgrading to the premium version.

### e. In-App Purchases (All My Gigs, Study With Me)

We use **Google Play Billing** to process premium upgrades, tips, and remove-ads purchases. Payment details are handled directly by Google and are not collected or stored by us.

### f. Study With Me — study content and device features

Study With Me is a personal study companion. Data you create (subjects, notes, flashcards, to-do items, quiz scores, focus-session history, study goals, and voice or photo study materials) is stored **on your device** using local app storage.

The app may request permissions so you can use optional tools:

| Permission | Purpose |
|------------|---------|
| Camera | Capture study photos |
| Microphone | Record voice notes |
| Storage / media | Save photos, recordings, and access music for the study music player |
| Internet | Built-in browser tool and optional online features |
| Notifications & alarms | Daily study reminders you configure |
| Advertising ID | AdMob banner ads (unless removed via purchase) |

**Optional AI summarization:** If you turn on AI mode and provide a Google Gemini API key, text you submit for summarization is sent to **Google’s Generative Language API** to generate summaries. We do not receive or store that text on our servers. You control whether this feature is enabled and which API key is used.

We do not sell your study content.

### g. VitalSyncFlow — clinical workflow data

VitalSyncFlow is a nurse-assistant app for shift workflow support. Data you enter (patient identifiers, room numbers, vitals, shift notes, templates, reminders, and calculator inputs) is stored **locally on your device** unless you explicitly share it (for example, exporting or sharing a shift note through your device’s share sheet).

The app may request:

| Permission | Purpose |
|------------|---------|
| Microphone | Voice notes for nursing documentation |
| Storage | Saving exported files where supported |
| Internet | AdMob banner ads on the home screen |
| Network state | Checking connectivity for ads |

**You are responsible** for using VitalSyncFlow in compliance with your employer, facility, and applicable healthcare privacy rules (including HIPAA where applicable). parteSoft does not access, host, or process patient health information on our servers.

### h. Prayer Mate

Prayer Mate does not collect, store, or share any information and does not contain ads or third-party tracking.

## 3. How We Use Information

- **On-device processing** for app features (gig filtering, study tracking, vitals and notes, calculators, reminders, etc.).
- **Ad delivery** through Google AdMob where ads are shown.
- **In-app purchase processing** via Google Play Billing.
- **Optional third-party AI** (Study With Me only, when you enable it) via Google’s Gemini API.

---

## 4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data.

Third-party services that may process limited data:

| Service | Used by | Purpose |
|---------|---------|---------|
| Google AdMob | All My Gigs, Study With Me, VitalSyncFlow | Advertising |
| Google Play Billing | All My Gigs, Study With Me | Purchases and tips |
| Google Gemini API | Study With Me (optional, user-enabled) | AI text summarization |

Each third party has its own privacy policy.

---

## 5. Data Retention

We do not store personal data on parteSoft servers.

User preferences and app content remain on your device until you delete them or uninstall the app.

AdMob, Google Play Billing, and (if used) Google Gemini may retain data per their respective privacy policies.

---

## 6. Your Choices

- Disable **location**, **camera**, **microphone**, or other permissions in your device settings at any time (some features may stop working).
- **Remove ads** via in-app purchase (Study With Me) or premium upgrade (All My Gigs).
- **Turn off AI summarization** in Study With Me or remove your Gemini API key.
- **Uninstall** any app to delete locally stored data (except data retained by third parties per their policies).

---

## 7. Children’s Privacy

Our apps are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information through one of our apps, contact us and we will help you remove locally stored data by uninstalling the app.
